# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Casual retail investors evaluating a stock before buying. They want a quick, reliable answer without sifting through earnings calls or terminal screens. They arrive with a ticker in mind and mild uncertainty — looking for confidence to act.

## Product Purpose

StockBrewAI generates institutional-grade stock analysis reports in under 30 seconds for 50 cents each. It replaces hours of manual research with a single AI-powered workflow: enter a ticker, pick a report type, get a detailed breakdown. Success means the user reads the report, feels informed, and either acts on their conviction or saves the analysis for later.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, minimal, trustworthy. The interface earns credibility through restraint — no unnecessary decoration, no hype language, no gamification. It speaks like a competent analyst who respects your time: direct, precise, quietly confident.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI tools: gradient hero sections, sparkle icons, "powered by AI" badges, chat-bubble interfaces. StockBrewAI is a purpose-built analysis tool, not a chatbot wrapper.
- Gamified trading apps (Robinhood-style confetti, green/red dopamine triggers, social feeds). This is research, not entertainment.
- Overstuffed dashboards with 40 widgets competing for attention. One report, presented clearly.
- SaaS template aesthetics: identical card grids, hero-metric layouts, stock photography of people pointing at screens.

## Design Principles

1. **Expert confidence** — The interface should feel like it was built by people who understand finance, not people who understand landing page templates. Every element earns its place.
2. **Show the work** — Present analysis with enough depth that the user trusts the output. No black-box magic; structured, readable reports.
3. **Respect the decision** — The user is about to spend real money on a real stock. The design should support careful reading and clear thinking, never rush or pressure.
4. **One thing at a time** — Each screen has one job. The analysis form captures a ticker. The report presents findings. The dashboard organizes history. No feature creep per surface.
5. **Quiet authority** — Credibility through craft, not decoration. Typography, spacing, and hierarchy do the heavy lifting.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliance as baseline. Sufficient color contrast for data-heavy report content. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Standard best practices for keyboard navigation and screen reader support.
